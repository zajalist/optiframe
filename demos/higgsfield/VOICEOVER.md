# OptiFrame film — recording script

Cut length: **24.33 seconds**, 24 fps. Read in a calm, close, confident voice. Leave the gaps silent; the pictures carry them. No announcer emphasis and no background music.

| Time | Picture | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00.45–0:04.35 | Frame emerges from darkness | “The lenses you love should outlive their frame.” |
| 0:05.00–0:09.30 | Individual lenses catch and split light | “OptiFrame captures the shape of each lens.” |
| 0:09.85–0:14.25 | Finished frame comes into view | “Then shapes a new frame around the glass you already own.” |
| 0:14.70–0:19.15 | Detail of rims, bridge, and temples | “Choose the silhouette. Refine the fit. Lock each lens in place.” |
| 0:19.55–0:24.15 | Final frame and OptiFrame mark | “Try it on. Print a test kit. Keep the lenses. Change the frame.” |

Record the five lines in one quiet take, pausing between them. iPhone Voice Memos is fine. Keep the phone about 15–20 cm from your mouth and avoid rooms with echo. Send the unedited recording; timing and cleanup can be handled in the edit.
