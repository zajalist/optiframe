Capture. Check. Print.

  1. 01

    Capture each lens.

    Photograph each lens on the printed reference sheet. Capture the left and right lenses separately, with optical centres and top orientation marked by an eye-care provider.

    Print sheet
  2. 02

    Check the outlines.

    Review the proposed contours. Check dimensions with a ruler or calipers and an independent capture before using them to build a frame.

  3. 03

    Build around the wearer.

    Add pupil positions, fitting offsets, and lens edge thicknesses. Inspect the 3D assembly, then export individual STL parts or a single build plate.
