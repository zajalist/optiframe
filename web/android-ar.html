Does AR depth work here?

On an Android phone, open this page in Chrome. Tap Start, allow the camera, and slowly scan an opaque object for 15 seconds. No lens setup is needed for this first check.

What counts as working: the button becomes “Start 15-second AR scan,” the camera opens, the frame count rises, and you can download a nonempty PLY file after the scan. A camera launch alone does not prove depth is available.

Next: test a transparent lens
  1. Keep the lens upright in a fixed stand, with a patterned board behind it. Start 0.5–1 m away and move slowly in a small arc.
  2. Download both JSON and PLY after each scan. Repeat with the empty stand and record the phone model, lens, board offset and caliper measurements.
  3. Compare the lens and empty-stand clouds. Transparent lenses may return background depth. These points are experimental and do not feed the printable frame.

Capture stops after 15 seconds or 60 depth frames. Starting again replaces the previous capture, so save both files first.
