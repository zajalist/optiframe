EXPERIMENT / WEBXR DEPTH

Test phone depth.

Use Android Chrome on an ARCore depth supported phone, with Google Play Services for AR enabled. Open this page over HTTPS or forwarded localhost.

Transparent lenses may return the background depth. These points are experimental scene samples, never a measured lens outline or printable frame input. Continue with lens photos.
  1. Keep the lens upright in a fixed stand, with a patterned board behind it. First test an opaque object to check depth.
  2. Start 0.5–1 m away. Move the phone slowly in a small arc for 15 seconds.
  3. Download both files. Repeat with an empty stand; record the phone, lens, board offset and caliper measurements.
