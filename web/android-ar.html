Waiting for depth frames

What counts as working: depth frames accumulate, points appear in the 3D preview, and a nonempty PLY downloads. The percentage shows valid depth samples in the latest frame, not completeness of the object's surface.

Next: try a transparent lens
  1. Place the lens upright in a fixed stand with a patterned board behind it. Start 0.5–1 m away and move slowly.
  2. Save JSON and PLY after the scan. Repeat with the empty stand; record the phone model and lens dimensions.
  3. Compare both point clouds. A clear lens may return background depth. These points do not feed frame geometry yet.
Device and capture diagnostics
Scan stops after 15 seconds or 60 depth frames. Starting again replaces the previous capture, so save files first. The JSON contains raw depth and poses; PLY contains sparse sampled points. No RGB images are captured.
