Privacy.

The camera features work differently. The visual face try-on runs on your device; lens images are sent to the processing service when you use the scanner.

Account and waitlist data

If you create an account, we use your email and authentication data to sign you in. An access request records your selected phone platform, optional role and note, consent, status and timestamps. We use that information to review requests and communicate about access. Request details are visible to the OptiFrame reviewer and a notification is emailed to the project owner.

Browser storage holds the session needed for sign-in and may hold temporary scan, fit and style choices so you can move between steps. You can clear your browser’s site data to remove those local copies.

Camera and scan data

The sample-frame visual try-on and browser face estimate process front-camera frames on the device. They do not save camera frames to your account. Your confirmed measurements may be kept in browser storage for the fitting flow.

The lens scanner sends selected photos or camera frames to the OptiFrame processing service to find lens edges. Imported video is processed through a temporary file that the service removes after the request. Do not include unrelated people or sensitive documents in a lens image. The prototype has no account gallery for lens photos, but browser session data and server/provider request metadata can persist beyond a single scan.

Services involved

OptiFrame uses Supabase for account and access records, Resend for access-request notifications, and Vercel to serve the website. The lens-processing service runs separately on the project desktop. Those services may process technical request information needed to deliver the feature. We do not use face-camera frames to train a model.

Your choices and questions

You can browse the public site without creating an account, deny camera permission and use manual input where offered. To ask what account data is held, correct it, withdraw from the waitlist or request deletion, email zejbadr@gmail.com. We will review the request and explain any records we must retain. We have not set a fixed deletion schedule for early-access account records yet.

We will update this page when data handling changes. The current date above tells you which version you are reading.
