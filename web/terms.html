Terms of service.

OptiFrame is an early-access design tool for fitting a new experimental frame around existing lenses. Here is the short, plain-language agreement for using it.

The preview is free

OptiFrame does not charge you to use this early-access preview. Your own printing materials, equipment, connectivity, and any professional eye-care services are separate. Features and availability may change during development.

Make, check, then wear

The generated outlines, visual try-on and CAD files are prototype aids. A phone photo or face estimate does not check a prescription, prove lens edge accuracy, or certify that a printed frame is safe to wear.

Before wearing a frame, have a qualified eye-care professional confirm lens suitability, optical centres, orientation and fit. Verify the printed scale, physical lens retention and finished parts. Do not rely on an unverified preview for vision, driving, protective eyewear or other safety-sensitive use.

Your account and access

You may browse public pages and try sample frames without an approved account. Some scanning and export features require sign-in and individual approval.
